# Anti-Slop AI Design & Engineering Rules

1. **Authentic Content Over Marketing Fluff**:
   - Do NOT invent fake stats (e.g. "50,000+ users", "10x speed"). Use real contract facts, verified chain parameters (Arc Testnet 5042002, USDC native gas, ERC-8183, ERC-8004), and tangible explanations.
   - Ban cliché tropes like spamming sparkles or empty buzzwords.

2. **No Empty 1-Screen Login Walls**:
   - Users must be able to explore the platform, browse public escrows, test simulations, and read how it works BEFORE connecting a wallet.
   - The landing page must be rich, substantive, multi-section, and functional.

3. **Typography & Styling Discipline**:
   - No italic headings (`font-style: normal` always).
   - High contrast text (`text-slate-100`, `text-slate-300`).
   - Use tabular monospace formatting for addresses, hashes, and amounts.

4. **Interactive Completeness**:
   - Provide visual feedback for all 8 states (default, hover, focus, active, disabled, loading, error, success).
